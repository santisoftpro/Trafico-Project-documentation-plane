# 1. Purpose

This document defines the functional vision of Trafico.

It translates business objectives into product requirements that guide design, engineering, testing, and deployment.

The PRD serves as the primary reference for everyone involved in product development.

---

# 2. Product Overview

Trafico is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that enables intermediary businesses to securely coordinate WhatsApp conversations between customers and suppliers without exposing either party's identity.

The platform centralizes communication, prevents customer bypass, improves operational visibility, and strengthens business relationships.

---

# 3. Product Objectives

The product aims to:

- Protect intermediary businesses.
- Centralize conversations.
- Improve operator productivity.
- Prevent customer leakage.
- Provide branded communication.
- Deliver operational analytics.
- Support multiple businesses through a scalable SaaS architecture.

---

# 4. Target Users

The primary users include:

• Business Owners

• Operators

• Customers

• Suppliers

• System Administrators

---

# 5. Core Features

The first release will include:

- Company Management
- User Management
- Authentication
- WhatsApp Integration
- Customer Management
- Supplier Management
- Conversation Management
- Real-Time Messaging
- Identity Masking
- Leak Detection
- Notifications
- Analytics Dashboard

---

# 6. User Problems

The product solves the following problems:

- Customer bypass
- Manual message forwarding
- Fragmented communication
- Limited visibility
- Weak branding
- Lack of analytics

---

# 7. Product Success

The product is successful when businesses can securely coordinate customer and supplier communication without exposing identities while improving operational efficiency.

---

# 8. Product Constraints

The product depends on:

- WhatsApp Business Platform
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Internet Connectivity
- Secure Authentication
