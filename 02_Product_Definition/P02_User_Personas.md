# 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary user groups of Trafico. Understanding user personas ensures the platform is designed around real user needs, responsibilities, and goals.

---

# 2. Persona Overview

Trafico serves five primary user groups:

- Business Owner
- Operator
- Customer
- Supplier
- System Administrator

Each persona interacts with the platform differently and has unique responsibilities.

---

# 3. Business Owner

## Description

The Business Owner manages the company using Trafico.

Examples:

- Travel Agency Owner
- Recruitment Agency
- Software Agency
- Real Estate Company

## Goals

- Protect customer relationships
- Monitor business performance
- Manage employees
- View analytics
- Grow the business

## Responsibilities

- Create company
- Manage subscription
- Invite employees
- Configure settings
- Review reports

## Frustrations

- Losing customers
- Poor visibility
- Slow operations
- Employee mistakes

---

# 4. Operator

## Description

Employees responsible for managing customer conversations.

## Goals

- Respond quickly
- Assign suppliers
- Resolve customer requests
- Keep conversations organized

## Responsibilities

- Receive conversations
- Forward messages
- Assign suppliers
- Monitor conversations
- Close conversations

## Frustrations

- Too many conversations
- Manual message forwarding
- No search tools
- Repetitive work

---

# 5. Customer

## Description

A person requesting a service.

## Goals

- Receive quick responses
- Complete bookings
- Communicate easily

## Responsibilities

- Send requests
- Provide information
- Confirm services

## Frustrations

- Slow replies
- Confusing communication
- Delayed updates

---

# 6. Supplier

## Description

A business or individual delivering the requested service.

## Goals

- Receive work opportunities
- Respond quickly
- Complete jobs

## Responsibilities

- Review requests
- Provide quotations
- Deliver services

## Frustrations

- Missing information
- Delayed communication
- Unclear customer requirements

---

# 7. System Administrator

## Description

Responsible for platform administration.

## Goals

- Maintain uptime
- Secure the platform
- Monitor infrastructure

## Responsibilities

- Monitor servers
- Manage infrastructure
- Review logs
- Handle incidents

---

# 8. Summary

Each user persona has different goals and workflows. Understanding these differences ensures Trafico delivers a user experience tailored to every participant.
