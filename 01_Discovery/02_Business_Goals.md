# Business Goals

**Project Name:** Trafico

**Version:** 1.0


**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business objectives that Trafico aims to achieve. These goals guide product strategy, engineering decisions, feature prioritization, and long-term product growth.

---

# 2. Primary Business Goal

Enable intermediary businesses to securely coordinate communication between customers and suppliers while preventing disintermediation and maintaining ownership of customer relationships.

---

# 3. Strategic Business Goals

## Goal 1 — Protect Business Relationships

Provide a communication platform that prevents customers and suppliers from exchanging direct contact information, ensuring future business continues through the intermediary.

---

## Goal 2 — Strengthen Brand Trust

Deliver a professional and branded communication experience where customers interact with the business rather than individual suppliers.

---

## Goal 3 — Centralize Communication

Replace fragmented WhatsApp conversations with a single platform that provides complete visibility and operational control.

---

## Goal 4 — Improve Operational Efficiency

Reduce manual coordination by allowing operators to manage conversations, suppliers, and customers from one centralized dashboard.

---

## Goal 5 — Build a Scalable SaaS Platform

Develop a multi-tenant platform capable of serving businesses across multiple industries without requiring significant architectural changes.

---

## Goal 6 — Generate Recurring Revenue

Provide a subscription-based pricing model that generates predictable monthly recurring revenue while supporting business growth.

---

## Goal 7 — Support Multiple Industry Verticals

Create a reusable communication engine that can be adapted for industries such as:

- Travel
- Software Agencies
- Design Studios
- Real Estate
- Home Services
- Logistics
- Recruitment

without rebuilding the core platform.

---

# 4. Business Objectives

The project should enable businesses to:

- Reduce customer leakage.
- Increase customer retention.
- Improve supplier coordination.
- Deliver faster response times.
- Improve operational transparency.
- Strengthen brand credibility.
- Scale operations with minimal additional staff.

# 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)

The success of Trafico may be measured using the following indicators:

### Customer Success

- Customer retention rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Average response time
- Conversation completion rate

---

### Business Performance

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Active subscriptions
- Number of active companies
- Average conversations per company
- Customer acquisition rate

---

### Operational Performance

- Average operator workload
- Supplier response time
- Number of blocked leak attempts
- Conversation resolution time

---

### Platform Performance

- Platform uptime
- Message delivery success rate
- API availability
- Average message processing time

# 6. Long-Term Business Vision

The long-term objective is to establish Trafico as the communication infrastructure for intermediary businesses worldwide by providing secure, scalable, and industry-specific conversation management solutions.
