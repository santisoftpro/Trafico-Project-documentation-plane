**Project Name:** Trafico

**Version:** 1.0


**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document identifies all stakeholders involved in the Trafico project, defines their responsibilities, expectations, and influence on the project.

Understanding stakeholders ensures that business requirements, technical decisions, and project priorities align with the needs of everyone involved.

---

# 2. Stakeholder Overview

The Trafico ecosystem consists of multiple stakeholders who interact with the platform in different ways.

Each stakeholder has unique responsibilities, goals, and expectations.

---

# 3. Primary Stakeholders

## 3.1 Business Owner

### Description

The owner of the intermediary business using Trafico.

Examples include:

- Travel Agency Owner
- Software Agency Founder
- Real Estate Broker
- Service Company Owner

### Responsibilities

- Register the company.
- Manage subscriptions.
- Configure business settings.
- Invite employees.
- Monitor business performance.
- Define operational policies.

### Goals

- Protect customer relationships.
- Increase operational efficiency.
- Maintain brand professionalism.
- Prevent customer leakage.

---

## 3.2 Operator

### Description

Employees responsible for managing customer conversations.

### Responsibilities

- Handle incoming conversations.
- Assign suppliers.
- Monitor active chats.
- Respond to customer requests.
- Review leak detection alerts.
- Close completed conversations.

### Goals

- Respond quickly.
- Resolve customer requests.
- Coordinate suppliers efficiently.

---

## 3.3 Customer

### Description

End-user requesting a product or service.

### Responsibilities

- Submit inquiries.
- Exchange information.
- Receive updates.

### Goals

- Fast responses.
- Professional communication.
- Reliable service.

---

## 3.4 Supplier

### Description

Business or individual delivering the requested service.

Examples include:

- Driver
- Hotel
- Freelancer
- Contractor
- Property Owner

### Responsibilities

- Respond to requests.
- Provide quotations.
- Deliver services.

### Goals

- Receive work opportunities.
- Communicate efficiently.
- Complete assignments successfully.
