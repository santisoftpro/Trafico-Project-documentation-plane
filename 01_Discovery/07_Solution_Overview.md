# 1. Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the proposed Trafico solution and explains how it addresses the business problems identified during the Discovery phase.

---

# 2. Solution Overview

Trafico is a cloud-based communication management platform that enables intermediary businesses to securely coordinate conversations between customers and suppliers while maintaining complete operational control.

The platform acts as a secure communication layer that relays messages, protects participant identities, and provides businesses with centralized visibility into every conversation.

---

# 3. Core Capabilities

The platform will provide the following capabilities:

- Secure message relay
- Identity masking
- Conversation management
- Operator dashboard
- Supplier assignment
- Centralized message history
- Leak detection
- Business analytics
- Multi-company support
- Role-based access control

---

# 4. Solution Workflow

1. Customer contacts the business through WhatsApp.
2. Trafico receives the message.
3. The conversation is created or updated.
4. An operator reviews or manages the request.
5. A supplier is assigned.
6. Messages are securely relayed.
7. Trafico monitors communication for policy violations.
8. The conversation is completed and archived.

---

# 5. Expected Business Benefits

Businesses using Trafico should be able to:

- Protect customer relationships.
- Improve communication efficiency.
- Reduce manual coordination.
- Increase operational transparency.
- Deliver consistent branded communication.
- Scale customer operations more effectively.

# 6. High-Level Features

The initial version of Trafico is expected to include:

# 7. Success Vision

The success of Trafico will be measured by its ability to become the trusted communication platform for intermediary businesses while reducing customer leakage and improving operational efficiency.

---

# 8. Summary

Trafico is designed to transform fragmented, manual communication processes into a centralized, secure, and scalable communication platform that protects business relationships and supports long-term growth.
