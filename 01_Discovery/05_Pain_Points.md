# 1. Purpose

This document identifies the operational, business, and technical challenges experienced by intermediary businesses using traditional communication methods.

Understanding these pain points ensures that Trafico addresses real business problems rather than simply introducing new technology.

---

# 2. Business Challenges

Businesses that coordinate communication between customers and suppliers face several recurring challenges that reduce efficiency, profitability, and customer retention.

---

# 3. Identified Pain Points

## 3.1 Customer Disintermediation

### Description

Customers and suppliers exchange direct contact information and continue doing business without involving the intermediary.

### Business Impact

- Loss of recurring revenue
- Reduced customer retention
- Loss of long-term business relationships

---

## 3.2 Lack of Communication Control

### Description

Businesses cannot control how conversations continue once direct contact has been established.

### Business Impact

- Reduced visibility
- Inconsistent customer experience
- Difficult quality assurance

---

## 3.3 Fragmented Conversations

### Description

Business communication is spread across multiple employee WhatsApp accounts and personal devices.

### Business Impact

- Difficult conversation tracking
- No centralized history
- High dependency on individual employees

---

## 3.4 Limited Operational Visibility

### Description

Managers cannot easily monitor active conversations or employee performance.

### Business Impact

- Slow decision making
- Difficult supervision
- Reduced operational transparency

---

## 3.5 Manual Coordination

### Description

Operators manually copy messages between customers and suppliers.

### Business Impact

- Time-consuming
- Increased human error
- Slow response times

---

## 3.6 Brand Inconsistency

### Description

Customers often interact with individual suppliers instead of the business itself.

### Business Impact

- Weak brand recognition
- Reduced customer trust
- Poor customer experience

---

## 3.7 Lack of Analytics

### Description

Businesses have limited insight into operational performance.

### Business Impact

- No performance metrics
- Poor strategic planning
- Difficult capacity management

---

| Pain Point | Business Impact | Priority |
|------------|----------------|----------|
| Customer bypass | High | Critical |
| Manual coordination | High | High |
| Fragmented communication | High | High |
| Limited visibility | Medium | High |

# 5. Summary

The current communication process introduces operational inefficiencies, reduces customer retention, limits business visibility, and exposes intermediary businesses to customer leakage.

These challenges represent the primary problems Trafico is intended to solve.
