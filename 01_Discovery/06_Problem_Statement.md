# 1. Purpose

This document formally defines the core business problem Trafico is designed to solve.

A clearly defined problem statement ensures that all future product decisions remain focused on solving the right problem.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Intermediary businesses rely on communication platforms such as WhatsApp to coordinate services between customers and suppliers.

These platforms expose participant identities, allowing customers and suppliers to establish direct relationships outside the intermediary's control.

As a result, businesses lose future revenue, reduce customer retention, and struggle to maintain ownership of customer relationships.

In addition, manual message forwarding, fragmented conversations, and limited operational visibility create inefficiencies that reduce service quality and increase operational costs.

---

# 3. Root Causes

The primary causes of the problem include:

- Exposure of participant identities.
- Lack of centralized communication.
- Manual coordination processes.
- No conversation management tools.
- Limited operational monitoring.
- Absence of communication analytics.

---

# 4. Business Impact

Without an effective communication management platform, businesses may experience:

- Customer leakage.
- Revenue loss.
- Reduced operational efficiency.
- Poor customer experience.
- Weak brand identity.
- Limited scalability.

---

# 5. Desired Outcome

Businesses require a communication platform that:

- Protects customer relationships.
- Prevents direct contact between participants.
- Centralizes communication.
- Improves operational visibility.
- Supports scalable business growth.

---

# 6. Summary

The primary problem is not communication itself.

The primary problem is the inability of intermediary businesses to maintain ownership, visibility, and control over customer relationships while coordinating services between multiple parties.
