---

# 1. Purpose

This document identifies potential risks that could impact the successful delivery or operation of the Trafico platform.

---

# 2. Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|----|------|------------|--------|------------|
| R001 | Meta Business verification delayed | Medium | High | Begin verification early |
| R002 | WhatsApp API downtime | Low | High | Queue messages and implement retries |
| R003 | Scope creep | High | High | Formal change request process |
| R004 | Message delivery failures | Medium | High | Monitoring and retry mechanisms |
| R005 | Data loss | Low | Critical | Automated backups |
| R006 | Security breach | Low | Critical | Encryption, RBAC, audit logs |
| R007 | Infrastructure outage | Medium | High | Redundant hosting and monitoring |
| R008 | Third-party API pricing changes | Medium | Medium | Review pricing periodically |

---

# 3. Risk Review

Project risks should be reviewed:

- During sprint planning
- Before major releases
- During project review meetings

---

# 4. Summary

Risk management is an ongoing activity throughout the project lifecycle. New risks should be documented and reviewed as the project evolves.
