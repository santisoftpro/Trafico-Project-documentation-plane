Project: Trafico

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the measurable outcomes that determine whether the Trafico project has successfully achieved its objectives.

---

# 2. Business Success

The project will be considered successful if:

- Businesses can manage customer and supplier conversations from one platform.
- Customer leakage is significantly reduced.
- Operators can efficiently coordinate conversations.
- Businesses maintain visibility over all communication.

---

# 3. Product Success

The platform should:

- Successfully relay messages.
- Protect participant identities.
- Detect prohibited contact sharing.
- Store conversation history.
- Support multiple businesses.

---

# 4. Technical Success

The platform should achieve:

- High system availability.
- Reliable message delivery.
- Secure authentication.
- Scalable architecture.
- Responsive user interface.

---

# 5. User Success

Users should be able to:

- Learn the platform with minimal training.
- Complete common tasks efficiently.
- Access conversation history easily.
- Receive timely notifications.

---

# 6. Operational Success

The platform should:

- Reduce manual coordination.
- Improve response times.
- Provide actionable analytics.
- Support business growth.

---

# 7. Summary

The success of Trafico will be measured not only by technical delivery but also by its ability to improve business operations and protect intermediary relationships.
