# 1. Purpose

This document defines the boundaries of the Trafico project by specifying what will be included and excluded from the initial product release.

Clearly defining the project scope helps align stakeholder expectations, reduce project risk, and prevent uncontrolled feature expansion.

---

# 2. Project Scope

The initial release of Trafico will provide a secure communication platform that enables intermediary businesses to manage WhatsApp conversations between customers and suppliers while protecting participant identities.

---

# 3. In Scope

The following features are included in the first release.

## Company Management

- Company registration
- Company profile management
- Business settings
- Subscription management

---

## User Management

- User authentication
- Role-based access control
- Employee invitations
- User permissions

---

## Customer Management

- Customer records
- Customer history
- Conversation history

---

## Supplier Management

- Supplier records
- Supplier assignment
- Supplier status management

---

## Conversation Management

- Create conversations
- Assign operators
- Assign suppliers
- Conversation history
- Archive conversations
- Conversation search

---

## Messaging

- WhatsApp message relay
- Text messages
- Images
- Documents
- Voice notes
- Message status

---

## Identity Protection

- Identity masking
- Contact information filtering
- Leak detection
- Blocked message logging

---

## Dashboard

- Active conversations
- Pending conversations
- Operator activity
- Analytics
- Notifications

---

## Reporting

- Conversation statistics
- Operator performance
- Leak reports
- Business metrics

---

# 4. Out of Scope

The following items are excluded from the initial release.

- Mobile application
- Voice calling
- Video calling
- Payment processing
- CRM functionality
- Accounting features
- AI chatbot
- Social media integrations
- Email marketing
- Workflow automation
- Multi-language AI assistant

These may be considered for future releases.

---

# 5. Future Enhancements

Potential future capabilities include:

- AI conversation summaries
- AI leak detection
- CRM integrations
- Public API
- Mobile applications
- Multi-channel messaging
- Workflow automation
- Customer satisfaction surveys

---

# 6. Scope Assumptions

The project assumes:

- Customers communicate through WhatsApp.
- Suppliers communicate through WhatsApp.
- Businesses have internet connectivity.
- Meta WhatsApp Business API is available.

---

# 7. Summary

This document establishes the agreed boundaries for the initial Trafico release and serves as the baseline for future planning and change management.
