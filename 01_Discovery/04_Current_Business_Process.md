# Current Business Process

**Project Name:** Trafico

**Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document describes how intermediary businesses currently manage communication between customers and suppliers before implementing Trafico.

Understanding the existing workflow helps identify inefficiencies, pain points, and opportunities for improvement.

---

# 2. Current Workflow

Most intermediary businesses currently coordinate services using WhatsApp.

Communication typically follows this sequence:

1. Customer contacts the business.
2. Business receives the request.
3. Business contacts one or more suppliers.
4. Supplier provides pricing or availability.
5. Business forwards responses to the customer.
6. Customer and supplier continue communicating.
7. Service is completed.

# 4. Existing Communication Channels

Businesses commonly rely on:

- WhatsApp
- Phone Calls
- SMS
- Email
- Manual Notes
- Excel Spreadsheets

Communication is often fragmented across multiple tools.

---

# 5. Current Challenges

The existing process presents several operational challenges:

- Customer and supplier identities become exposed.
- Conversations are scattered across employee devices.
- Limited visibility into ongoing operations.
- No centralized conversation history.
- Difficult to monitor employee performance.
- High risk of customer bypass.
- Inconsistent customer experience.

---

# 6. Manual Activities

Current operations often require employees to manually:

- Forward messages.
- Copy quotations.
- Track conversations.
- Contact suppliers.
- Update customers.
- Record completed jobs.

These manual processes increase workload and the likelihood of human error.

# 8. Business Pain Points

The current workflow results in:

- Delayed responses.
- Repetitive manual work.
- Poor conversation tracking.
- Lack of operational transparency.
- Customer leakage.
- Brand inconsistency.

---

# 9. Improvement Opportunities

An improved solution should:

- Centralize communication.
- Protect participant identities.
- Automate message routing.
- Improve operational visibility.
- Maintain branded communication.
- Record conversation history.
- Reduce manual coordination.

---

# 10. Summary

The current business process depends heavily on manual coordination through WhatsApp and other communication channels.

While this approach enables businesses to operate, it introduces operational inefficiencies, limits visibility, and exposes customer relationships.

These limitations provide the foundation for the Trafico platform and justify the need for a centralized communication management solution.
